# Viewer usability study — 10–12 minutes

Updated October 12, 2026 for the application after PR #33. This is the current protocol; its release-folder location is retained so existing links keep working. **Study prepared; human validation pending. No participants contacted and no participant responses collected.** [Instruction validation](VIEWER-TEST-VALIDATION.md) is an assistant walkthrough, not a usability result.

## Purpose and participants

Find out whether viewers can explain a match development, interpret a guided answer and its limits, inspect supporting events, and choose an explanation level independently of recap length. This evaluates the offline experience, not whether AI outperforms it.

Plan six individual sessions: three casual viewers and three knowledgeable viewers. Casual viewers watch occasionally and prefer everyday explanations; knowledgeable viewers watch regularly and are comfortable discussing tactics and statistics. Ask how often they watch and how comfortable they feel explaining a match (1–5); use their self-description, not a football quiz. Record mixed familiarity as described rather than forcing a category. If fewer sessions are practical, include at least one of each group and report the actual count.

The owner/facilitator arranges participants; this protocol does not authorize outreach. Use anonymous IDs, ask permission to take notes, and do not record audio/video by default. Keep actual responses outside this public repository. Use participants' usual device where practical, record device/browser and any accessibility needs, and include mobile use across the planned sessions. This small formative sample cannot establish population-wide preferences.

4. Rehearse the tasks below once, keeping the answer key out of the participant's view. For **70:00**, pause playback, drag **Match timeline** near the target, then focus the slider and use left/right arrows for one-second adjustments. ## Introduction — 1 minute

Read: “We are checking whether this app is clear, not testing your football knowledge. This is a fictional match. Please think aloud as you try each task, and tell me when something is unclear. You can skip a task or stop whenever you want. May I take anonymous notes?”

Read only each quoted task prompt below. Do not explain the controls or desired conclusions first. Start timing after the prompt; stop when the participant finishes or the task limit is reached. Ask “What are you looking for?” if they fall silent. Log any help. If they ask for help before the limit, offer one navigation hint and mark completion as prompted if they finish. At the time limit, record incomplete work as F; any later guided completion belongs in the post-help notes. Keep comprehension before help separate from any answer after help. If the app fails, record a technical block and skip dependent tasks rather than scoring the participant as incorrect. Save teaching or corrections until the end unless needed to proceed, in which case record them.

## Facilitator answer key — do not read aloud

These are scoring anchors, not sample participant responses. Accept accurate everyday paraphrases; exact terminology or memorized numbers are not required if the participant correctly distinguishes the comparisons. A fluent repetition without understanding is not full comprehension.

| Task | Expected path and full-comprehension anchor (2)                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                           |
| ---- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| T1   | Harbor leads 1–0 at 63:24. Cumulative statistics describe totals; those totals alone do not establish the timing, cause or chance quality of a recent change. Treat this as a baseline, not a requirement to discover the hidden story.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                   |
| T2   | Match centre → **What changed**, **Why it matters**, **What challenges it** and **Look closer**. Harbor's shots rise **0 → 4** across equal **9m12s** spells (45:00–54:12 and 54:12–63:24); none of those four scored. More shots do not establish better chances, dominance or a tactical cause. **Are these chances actually better?** is an unavailable explanation at this checkpoint: the earlier spell has no shots, so average quality cannot be compared. “Unavailable” does not mean the chances are worse.                                                                                                                                                                                                                      |
| T3   | Look closer → **What best describes this shot surge?**. Direct answer: **No single explanation is established by these events.** The comparison has shots **0 → 4**, shots after qualifying ball wins **0 → 0**, separate linked spells **0 → 0**, opponent shots **0 → 0**. The candidate explanations are **Not established**. Missing links do not prove another route, and sequence does not prove cause. **Verified event analysis** / **No model investigation** means deterministic checks, not a live AI answer. Full comprehension includes the conclusion, one supporting measure/limit, and correct source attribution.                                                                                                        |
| T4   | At 70:00 → **Has this increase already faded?** → **Yes, the increase has eased in the latest half of this spell.** Wider equal **12m30s** windows (45:00–57:30 and 57:30–70:00) show **1 → 4** shots; the current window's two **6m15s** halves show **3 → 1**. The recent slowdown does not erase the wider increase or predict the next goal.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                          |
| T5   | Within the fading answer → **Explore answer evidence** → e.g. **59:09 · shot · Replay**. The schematic replays synthetic recorded actions and endpoints; it is not broadcast footage, continuous player tracking, off-ball movement or proof of causation. The shot's passage begins at 58:39; the main match clock stays **70:00** while the separate replay clock advances. An event illustrates part of the evidence, not the whole aggregate comparison.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                              |
| T6   | **Catch me up** → **Explanation level**; then **Quick recap / Full recap**. Casual Viewer uses everyday wording; Football Fan adds match context and named contributions from verified events; Advanced Analysis includes more precise measurements and technical detail. They share the verified facts and limits, although not every detail appears in every level. Quick/Full independently changes recap breadth and number of moments. At 70:00 all combinations retain Harbor **1–0**, the wider shot increase and its recent weakening. Full comprehension distinguishes these two controls and preserves the development/qualification across levels. Any preferred level, including none, is valid for either familiarity group. |

If needed after an initial attempt, navigation hints are: T2 “Try Match centre”; T3 “Look under Look closer for the shot-surge question”; T4 “Try the question about whether the increase has faded”; T5 “Expand Explore answer evidence”; T6 “Use Explanation level, then the recap tabs.” Record the exact hint and time. Never supply the answer and count it as independent comprehension.

## Recording and debrief — 1 minute

Use the [session sheet](VIEWER-TEST-RECORD.md). Record each task separately:

- **Completion:** U = completed unaided; P = completed with a hint; F = not completed within the limit or gave up; X = not attempted/technical block (include reason). Routine checkpoint preparation is not a hint. Completion measures reaching/using the requested view; it does not imply understanding.
- **Comprehension before help:** 0 = incorrect or contradictory; 1 = partially correct or misses a material limitation; 2 = meets the task anchor; blank = not observed. Record any post-help score separately. Do not score missing data as zero or combine baseline T1 with later tasks into an “improvement” total.
- **Confusion:** 0 = none observed; 1 = hesitation/unclear term but continues; 2 = wrong interpretation, blocked action or help needed; blank = not observed. Note the screen/term, actual words and what happened. An observed hesitation is not proof of what the participant thought.

Ask: “What helped you understand the match most? What was confusing or missing? What one change would you make?” Record actual words, preferred level and reason, plus any difference between stated preference and observed comprehension. Leave unknown fields blank; do not invent quotes or completed sessions.

After real sessions, summarize per-task U/P/F/X counts and comprehension by familiarity group, with denominators and missing data shown. List recurring confusion, affected participant IDs, relevant quotes and proposed changes separately from observations. Prioritize a false conclusion about quality, causation, AI source or future outcomes, then blocked tasks, then wording preferences. Rehearse any revision and retest the affected tasks. Do not claim statistical significance, causal improvement over statistics, AI benefit or a winning level from this small ordered study. Until real sessions exist, report **0 sessions; human validation pending**, with no outcome tables populated.

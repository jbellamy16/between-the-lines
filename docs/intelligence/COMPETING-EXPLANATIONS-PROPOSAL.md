# Proposal: investigate the shape of a shot surge

Status: design and evaluation proposal only, October 12, 2026. No feature, prompt, fixture, provider configuration or live campaign is implemented by this PR.

## Decision

Investigate one question about an existing shot-increase story: **“What best describes this spell: a repeated recovery-to-shot route, a brief cluster of attempts, or both teams becoming more active?”** These explanations can coexist. The answer describes recorded play, never why a coach changed tactics or what caused the shots.

The opportunity is adaptive selection of evidence that changes a viewer's interpretation of a meaningful development. It is not asking the model to calculate a statistic or repeat a known verdict. Treat useful AI contribution as an unproven hypothesis: if a deterministic policy using the same evidence performs as well, ship that policy and retain the no-call path.

These studies are small synthetic/diagnostic samples, and their assistant assessments are not human preference evidence. Later guided success does not validate the generic Director's multi-turn path.

Fresh read-only reconstruction of the bundled `pressure` fixture confirms a useful negative control. At **63:24**, Harbor has 0 → 4 shots across `(45:00,54:12]` and `(54:12,63:24]`, but **zero** qualifying attacking-third recovery/shot links. At **70:00**, the shifted equal windows show 1 → 4 shots, current halves 3 → 1, and only **one of four** shots linked to such a recovery. The current assessment already exposes these facts. Neither state demonstrates repeated recovery-led attacks, and neither should be repackaged as model discovery. Keep these fixtures unchanged.

The narrower untested distinction is whether several linked shots represent several separate recorded possessions or repeated attempts within one possession, and how that distinction changes the interpretation alongside timing and opposing activity. The present `high-recovery-linked-shots` measurement counts shots, not distinct recovery-linked possessions across both windows. `get_recorded_sequence` can inspect one anchor, but there is no complete paired-window summary of this distinction. New deterministic evidence primitives would enable the investigation; their value must be separated from the model's contribution.

Quality-only questions are already answered. Substitution causality, pressing intensity and formations lack the required tracking or counterfactual evidence. The selected scenario stays within supported event relationships and connects directly to an existing prominent story.

## Expected behavior

Entry point: one optional guided intent, provisionally `shot-surge-explanations`, attached to an eligible shot `activity-change` story in **Look closer**. Preserve the source story, score/goal priority, replay and current presentation styles. No autonomous new alerts or free-text chat in this increment.

1. Reconstruct the existing shot-increase candidate, exact cutoff and its adjacent equal same-period windows. Supply the current deterministic assessment and all three relevant guided answers, including missing-value qualifications. Never conceal already-computed evidence to manufacture a need for a call.
2. Skip inference when the story is ineligible, required source capabilities are absent, or the supplied evidence already settles the question. In particular, zero or one qualifying link cannot establish a repeated route. A simple existing count/quality/fading answer remains deterministic. Define and freeze the ambiguity gate before evaluation: at least two qualifying current links and an unresolved distinction between distinct possessions and a concentrated spell, after existing qualifications are applied.
3. For an eligible unresolved case, let the model choose a bounded evidence query and a typed purpose such as `test-route-repetition`, `test-concentration` or `test-opponent-response`. Purposes are audit labels, not private reasoning or proof of useful work. Obtain complete aggregates before selecting illustrative sequences.
4. Let the result change the next query: inspect a distinct linked possession, an unlinked shot that challenges the apparent route, or counter-evidence about timing/opponent activity. Stop if the existing evidence suffices or another request cannot fit. Do not force a second query for appearance, repeat supplied calculations, cherry-pick windows, or repair a rejected answer with extra calls.
5. Publish a brief comparison of supported interpretations with the decisive evidence and strongest material qualification. Allow `mixed`, `unresolved` and `no-added-insight`; do not force a winner. Keep all mandatory contrary evidence even when the model selects a favorable lead.

| Interpretation                           | Evidence that can support it                                                                                                            | Evidence that limits it                                                                                                                                   |
| ---------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Repeated recorded recovery-to-shot route | At least two distinct current possessions containing qualifying links, with their count higher than in the earlier equal window.        | Several shots after one recovery are one linked possession; unchanged earlier frequency limits a claim of emergence; a late slowdown limits continuation. |
| Attempts concentrated in a brief cluster | At least two shots and at least half the current shots in one possession, or all current shots in one fixed half of the current window. | Several linked possessions across both halves limit an exclusively isolated-burst interpretation. Concentration and a repeated route can both be true.    |
| Both teams increased shot activity       | Both teams' current shot totals exceed their own previous equal-window totals.                                                          | This limits a one-sided reading; it does not refute a recovery route or prove an “open game,” dominance or equal performance.                             |

These are proposed descriptive eligibility rules, not calibrated significance thresholds. “Repeated” means observed in multiple possessions, not a prediction that the pattern will continue. Missing possession identity is unknown, not a distinct possession; missing source xG is unknown quality, not poor quality. No recovery link does not identify the alternative attacking route.

Illustrative acceptance example, **not a current fixture or result**: shots rise 2 → 6; four current shots follow high recoveries, but three belong to one possession and one to another; the earlier window had one linked possession; current halves contain 5 → 1 shots; opponent shots rise 1 → 5. An acceptable answer says the recorded route recurred in two possessions, but the surge is concentrated and both teams became more active. It must not turn “four linked shots” into “four separate attacks,” sustained pressure or pressing success. A matched variant with the same totals but four distinct linked possessions spread across both halves must change the emphasis. If the baseline already communicates the same interpretation, there is no AI win.

## Proposed implementation boundary

Retain observer → bounded investigator → typed plan → independent verifier → constrained renderer. Extend the existing workflow; do not add agents, a new service, external retrieval, a new event feed, model upgrade or a parallel cache/accounting system.

The new aggregate must group by `(match, period, team, possessionId)`, count each shot once and count each linked possession once. Reuse the existing strict high-recovery link definition: recovery event, success not false, normalized x ≥ 66.7, same team/period/possession, preceding event order and at most 30 recorded seconds with adequate timestamp precision. Do not substitute the broader tackle/interception “high ball win” metric. Label generated possessions as synthetic rather than recorded real-world possessions.

Assign shots to the existing `(start,end]` windows; a linking recovery may precede the window boundary if it meets the existing sequence rule. Retain that context event explicitly, without counting it as an in-window recovery. Preserve the query's 1,800-second bound and disclose contextual sequence bounds. Do not infer unrecorded possession transitions, rebounds or restart types from adjacency. An unavailable possession ID or minute-only timing makes the corresponding link comparison incomplete; it cannot justify a negative conclusion. Missing recovery coordinates likewise prevent a complete high-recovery classification. Suppress a comparison whose complete references exceed current caps, rather than increasing those caps or extrapolating from the last 40 displayed events.

The final plan contains the story/intent identity, interpretation IDs with `supported`, `limited` or `unresolved` status, registered supporting/challenging measurement or relation IDs, and a decision (`publish-comparison`, `no-added-insight`, `unresolved`). The model supplies no numbers, causal verbs, arbitrary prose or confidence probabilities. The server validates statuses against explicit predicates, rejects absent/unretrieved references, and appends mandatory caveats independently of selection. Verification establishes evidential validity; it cannot establish that the editorial choice is better than a rule. That is an evaluation question.

Preserve all current controls: server-owned source identity and immutable cutoff; source permissions and restricted-data no-inference rule; fresh query replay; no future/raw provider payload/private reasoning; four tool calls including initial retrieval; at most three provider attempts including any currently permitted bounded 429 retry; 1,800 output tokens per request; 18-second transport and 55-second workflow deadline within the 70-second lease; existing payload/evidence caps, pacing, Redis quotas and fail-closed production behavior. Keep success-only caching, accurate fallback provenance and zero-call cache reuse. Provider/budget/cancellation failure retains the verified deterministic answer. Public and proactive inference settings remain unchanged.

## Evaluation that can reject the AI hypothesis

First build and verify evidence arithmetic offline. Then freeze source/prompt versions, fixture and baseline hashes, question/cutoff, allowed outcomes, required challenges and campaign order **before** any new provider result. Existing receipts, generators, ledgers and allowances remain untouched. The current pressure examples above are regression/no-call controls, not hand-adjusted positive examples.

Use four paired arms with identical canonical inputs, display length and qualifications:

| Arm                                                             | Purpose                                                                                                                                                                                                    |
| --------------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| A: current Director plus all three deterministic guided answers | Measures benefit over what the product already knows; do not compare only against the bare shot count.                                                                                                     |
| B: deterministic policy with the new evidence                   | Compute every permitted aggregate, apply the same interpretation predicates, show all material challenges, and select replay anchors with frozen rules. Isolates whether new code alone explains any gain. |
| C: model with a fixed retrieval sequence                        | Supply the same initial packet, then the new route summary and a fixed counter-evidence query, within identical limits. Isolates adaptive retrieval from model selection and extra data.                   |
| D: proposed adaptive investigation                              | Same primitives and budget; model chooses follow-up scope/anchor and when to stop.                                                                                                                         |

After separate development cases, freeze **24 held-out states**: four each for repeated routes, concentrated attempts, increased activity from both teams, mixed evidence, misleading/unchanged route frequency, and unavailable-or-already-settled/no-call controls. The first 20 must satisfy the frozen ambiguity gate; the last four must not. Include at least four independently selected permitted recorded matches among the 20 and label the remaining diagnostic/synthetic states. If suitable recorded data is unavailable, report that limitation and leave the generalization gate unmet; never upload restricted research data. Freeze all identities before running; do not replace inconvenient cases after observing results.

Prelabel 12 of the 20 ambiguous cases as discrimination cases: changing possession grouping, timing or opposing activity while holding the headline shot increase fixed should change the necessary emphasis or challenge. Include paired interventions, but count variants from one match as correlated, not independent matches. Reference outcomes must come from independent event arithmetic and an adjudicated football/editorial rubric, not another model's answer. Allow multiple acceptable interpretations and unresolved results where the data cannot distinguish them.

Offline adversarial coverage must include repeated possession IDs across halves/teams, multiple shots after one recovery, failed recoveries, tackle/recovery confusion, boundary-spanning context, missing locations/possession/xG, minute precision, truncation, wrong actors/IDs, injected source text, invented plans, reordered evidence, prefix invariance, rewind, stale cache, cancellation, rate limits and unknown usage. Scripted transports prove orchestration and verification only.

| Gate                                  | Predeclared acceptance target                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                  |
| ------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Safety and evidence                   | Zero published unsupported, causal, future or wrong-identity claims; 100% recomputable numbers/references/windows; all material caveats survive both styles and lengths. Any accepted violation blocks advancement. Rejected plans remain failures, not successes.                                                                                                                                             |
| No-call and fallback                  | All four no-call controls dispatch zero requests. Every scripted failure preserves the deterministic story and correct provenance. No failure result is cached as verified AI output.                                                                                                                                                                                                                          |
| Completion                            | At least 18/20 eligible live D cases yield verified results within unchanged limits; report all 20, including failures and unattempted cases. A safe fallback is not a model completion.                                                                                                                                                                                                                       |
| Discrimination                        | At least 10/12 prelabelled D cases include the necessary interpretation and strongest challenge, or the reference-required unresolved result. On at least 8/12, a retrieved fact changes/qualifies the initial assessment and appears in the final answer; redundant calls or restatements receive zero credit.                                                                                                |
| Increment over new deterministic code | Three independent blinded reviewers (two football-literate, one editorial) compare paired outputs. D must win by majority on at least 12/20 eligible states against B, and 14/20 against A, with no more than two losses against either. Ties, failures and unattempted cases stay in the denominator; missing reviews leave the gate unmet. Record reasons and disagreements.                                 |
| Adaptive value                        | Across the 12 discrimination cases, D must have at least three more successful interpretation-plus-challenge outcomes than C, using the same rubric, and no additional safety failures. Otherwise do not claim adaptive retrieval adds value.                                                                                                                                                                  |
| Practical cost and latency            | For D, observed median end-to-end latency ≤10 seconds and nearest-rank p95 ≤20 seconds across all started eligible workflows, including failures and pacing but excluding inter-case spacing. Conservatively accounted campaign spend / verified D completions ≤$0.03. Retain unknown-usage reserves. Report sample size and raw usage; these are pilot targets, not population reliability or invoice claims. |

A fact is incremental only if it was absent from arm A's full answer and changes a supported interpretation, material qualification or evidence choice. Newly computed possession arithmetic is credited to B as well as D. More queries, a longer answer, HTTP 200, verified references, and an assistant's preference do not establish model benefit. Count human judgments by state, with match-clustered uncertainty and explicit ties; this small pilot is a decision gate, not statistical proof of broad superiority.

## Implementation and live-study sequence

1. In a later PR, implement the deterministic evidence/verification and arm B first, with independently computed fixture expectations. If it resolves the scenario clearly enough, stop there and reject the paid feature.
2. Add the typed investigation variant and offline scripted branching/rejection tests. Re-run Director, guided, hypothesis, cache, accounting and browser regressions, typecheck and build; keep ordinary CI offline. Review payload size and capacity against the unchanged limits before live work.
3. Obtain a separate, explicit live-study authorization after freezing a reviewable candidate. A proposed maximum for both paid arms combined is **40 workflows, 120 actual HTTP attempts including retries, and US$2 conservatively reserved spend**, whichever is reached first. This ceiling is a proposal, not an allowance granted by this task. B and A remain zero-call; run no-call controls with transport interception to prove no dispatch.
4. Refresh model/deployment, price and remaining durable allowance read-only at preflight. Use a new manifest-bound ledger with reservation before every dispatch, full unknown-usage retention and at least the existing 65-second spacing. Do not reuse a consumed launcher, reset counters, raise quotas or enable hosted AI. If existing controls cannot support the plan, record blocked execution. Stop on terminal provider failure, verification inconsistency, accounting ambiguity or a ceiling; do not silently rerun or replace cases. Unattempted cases leave the acceptance denominator intact.
5. Save all arms, query purposes/traces, complete original outputs, verifications, latency, usage/reservations and case outcomes in a new dated directory. Collect blinded human reviews separately; do not fabricate reviewer scores. Advance only if every gate passes. If D beats A but not B, prefer the deterministic implementation. If D beats B but not C, the adaptive-investigation claim fails and a simpler fixed-retrieval proposal needs separate review. No outcome automatically authorizes public inference or deployment.

This PR supplies only the investigation design and a falsifiable evaluation plan. Useful model contribution, new multi-turn Foundry reliability and human preference remain unestablished.

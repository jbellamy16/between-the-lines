# Bounded investigator: orchestration contracts

October 12, 2026. Implements the first single-investigator loop from the [proposal](AGENTIC-INVESTIGATOR-PROPOSAL.md). **Only scripted responses ran. No paid model requests, live Foundry evaluation, Azure changes, public activation or UI changes. Actual model intelligence and incremental benefit remain unproven.**

## What is implemented

`runBoundedInvestigator` in `src/lib/ai/director/investigator.ts` returns the complete B2 answer by default, with zero provider dispatch. It is server-only and has no public-route or UI integration. Normal mode retains B2's sufficient, unresolved and no-material-gain outcomes. Inference cannot recover absent source data, and these development cases already have a strong deterministic answer.

The explicit `mode: "evaluation"` path runs one investigator through the existing Foundry Responses adapter. It still requires configured Foundry, permitted sources and ready usage controls. This internal switch is a comparison mechanism, **not a spending authorization or public feature flag**. The ordinary offline test command intercepts transport with dummy credentials; no executable live campaign is added.

The model receives the unchanged versioned evidence catalogue, B2's initial complete calculations/assessments/guided answers, mandatory qualifications and evidence references, followed by its selected results. It can choose another eligible focal development, a diagnostic catalogue option, up to three registered competing interpretation IDs, support/challenge emphasis, and a stopping decision. Initial event display rows are omitted to fit the existing token allowance, with explicit delivery coverage; all source calculations and references remain supplied. Follow-up bundles include their bounded event display rows. B1/B2 receive exactly their existing evidence and computation allowances.

The contract deliberately starts with **one focal story**, hence no multi-story ranking or overlap slots. The model can switch focus, but the new story needs its complete assessment and all applicable guided answers. A returned surge bundle satisfies its nested questions. An attempted duplicate, covered nested question or equivalent route aggregate already supplied by a guided bundle is rejected. There is no arbitrary tool/window/team/player/timestamp/statistic or model-written factual prose field.

`investigator-contract.ts` resolves ID-only selections against discovery 1.1.0. Each read carries the advertised purpose, focal ID and considered interpretation IDs. Team/development membership, availability and duplicate checks precede execution. Two adaptive retrieval rounds fit at most three requests when no retry occurs. Final selection is separately constrained by lead eligibility, explicit preferences/seen state and mandatory score/full-time priority.

Interpretation and support/challenge choices are a verified editorial sidecar. The returned `BroadcastStory` and guided answers retain **all** canonical evidence, alternatives, computed statuses, unknowns and qualifications, regardless of selected emphasis. Story wording and rendering remain deterministic. Stories retain provider `offline` because the facts/presentation are computed; the receipt separately attributes the selection to the model contract. No UI presents the new sidecar yet.

## Verification and stopping

The verifier creates a fresh B2 baseline and evidence reader from canonical input, verifies exact index and initial-bundle hashes, replays every selected option, and compares complete trace entries, resolved queries, evidence IDs, coverage and response digests. It then independently recomputes the selected hypothesis, validates the editorial plan and uses the existing story renderer. It does **not** require the model to copy B2's selection.

A selected answer must have a complete verified assessment and every applicable whole question bundle. A premature stop with missing mandatory evidence is rejected. Partial guided evidence or omitted catalogue entries require `unresolved`; unknown xG/possession/timing remain unknown. General off-ball/intent limitations remain attached to a supported descriptive answer. A settled empty catalogue can stop with `no-material-gain`; abstention cannot discard a supported B2 answer. `sufficient` is scoped to the available bounded catalogue, never exhaustive knowledge of play or proof of superior editorial judgment.

A model may finish immediately using supplied B2 evidence. When the last request is forced by attempt or token capacity, even a valid plan is marked **application/budget, voluntary false**. Provider, deadline, controls, budget and cancellation outcomes are distinct from model stopping decisions. Invalid outputs and failures preserve the current deterministic fallback. Cancellation returns no stories or answers.

## Controls, receipts and payload limits

The existing `controlledNarration` owns shared locks, workflow reservations, success-only caching and cancellation. `providerResponse` continues to reserve every HTTP attempt, including retries, through the existing Redis/local accounting path. No quotas, TLS rules, unknown-charge reserves, provider retries, price settings or deployment configuration change. Production still fails closed without its required Redis controls.

The loop enforces at most three HTTP attempts and two adaptive rounds, within the existing four-tool ceiling. The initial composed deterministic packet counts as one application tool invocation; at most two model reads follow. B2's preparatory local work and fresh verification are not relabeled as inference or restricted to imitate model tool costs. All attempts share the existing 55-second deadline, 18-second transport timeout, 1,800-output-token allowance and 131,072-byte payload ceiling. Lower configured token/request/durable limits win. Discovery retains its existing scope, 48,000-character response, 40-event display and whole-assessment limits.

`investigator-wire.ts` interns repeated strings, object shapes and subtrees in JSON tables. Round-trip tests verify lossless encoding, including source text resembling encoding tags. This compresses the supplied data without dropping calculations or qualifications. The independent verifier never trusts or decodes this model wire representation. A real model's ability to use it reliably is **unmeasured**.

Initial serialized provider payloads in the 12 scripted cases are recorded in the [snapshot](BOUNDED-INVESTIGATOR-SCRIPTED.json). These are actual request-byte measurements with mock deployment metadata, not measured tokens. The existing conservative token estimator includes output reserves; larger follow-ups can force a final-only request or prevent another dispatch. A smaller goal fixture demonstrates two retrieval rounds and three requests inside the existing allowance. This does not prove that every catalogue or investigation can fit. Oversized payloads or insufficient capacity fail closed; no mandatory evidence is trimmed to make a request succeed.

The decision receipt records versions, canonical digest/exact cutoff, initial evidence identity, initial and final selections, typed considered interpretations, resolved queries, evidence IDs, coverage, response digests, stop origin/reason, verification and fallback disposition, attempts, tool/round counts, usage completeness, cache status and maximum request bytes. It excludes credentials, raw provider errors, free-form explanations and private reasoning. Selected foreground references identify observable choices, not private thought.

Cache identity includes investigator/prompt/schema/catalogue/B2 versions, canonical index identity (source revision, exact cutoff, preferences and seen evidence), provider, model and endpoint. Cache hits are independently replayed before publication. Cancellation by one subscriber cannot mutate a continuing shared workflow's receipt. Rewind, source revision and preference changes do not reuse incompatible cached decisions. Failed/cancelled workflows do not populate the success cache. Cached receipt attempts describe the original generation, not new dispatches on the cache hit.

## Offline evaluation and its limits

Run `AI_ENABLED=false npm run eval:investigator`. Tests intercept `fetch` before enabling dummy Foundry settings, exercise the real provider wrapper with real local accounting, and write `artifacts/bounded-investigator/scripted.json`. The checked-in [snapshot](BOUNDED-INVESTIGATOR-SCRIPTED.json) includes input/source hashes and per-case receipts. Run `AI_ENABLED=false npm run eval:editorial` for the unchanged complete production/B1/B2 comparison.

The comparison uses the same 12 authored development cases as PR #39. B1 and B2 remain byte-for-byte unchanged. A simple authored control script chooses B2's supplied lead and orders registered interpretations; it has no case labels or future records. Its verified stories and complete answers equal B2 and B1 on all 12 cases. This is intentionally a strong mechanics control, **not an independent intelligent policy, held-out evaluation or AI advantage**. Normal-mode controls dispatch zero requests on all 12 cases; explicit evaluation intentionally makes one mocked decision per case, even on solved/no-call states.

Separate scripted attacks and branches test: relevant option selection; different temporal branches; switching to another verified story after targeted counter-evidence; competing interpretation order; voluntary and forced stopping; premature/unsupported stops; invented IDs/prose/statistics and arguments; duplicate/nested reads; unknown source capabilities; restricted sources and disabled/missing controls; lower request limits; 429 accounting/unknown usage; provider failure; source revision during a request; exact rewind/preferences/cache identity; cancellation and shared subscribers; and fresh-replay receipt tampering. All complete canonical answers are compared directly, not scored only by a caveat count.

The temporal scripts react to evidence already present in the shared summaries. Follow-up illustrations and reordering earn **zero factual-discovery credit**. Model decisions, comprehension of compact evidence, strongest-challenge quality, human preference, measured latency/spend, real-match generalization, adaptive advantage over fixed retrieval and advantage over B2 remain unproven. The proposed 36-slot holdout, blinded reviews, fixed-vs-adaptive model comparison and a separately authorized finite live campaign remain future work. No model-eligible production gate is claimed: this PR intentionally keeps normal operation deterministic.

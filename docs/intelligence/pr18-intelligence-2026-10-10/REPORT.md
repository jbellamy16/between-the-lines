# Real Foundry intelligence review — PR #18

**Verdict: PROMISING.** Between the Lines produces inspectable, qualified football observations, but this evaluation does not establish exceptional model-directed intelligence or better editorial judgment than the pre-PR Director. The current deployment is not reliable enough for an unattended live demonstration of the larger investigations.

Evaluated October 10, 2026 (America/Chicago): PR #18 head `22fb00517a38a8abadf00ae19d299a74f01d1fa0`, Director `1.2.0`, existing `between-the-lines-gpt54-mini`, GPT-5.4 Mini `2026-03-17`, GlobalStandard. PR #18 was open, not assumed merged. Product source, model, generator, verification boundaries, production spending settings and Azure quotas remained unchanged. Nothing was merged or deployed.

## What the live evidence says

- **18 primary states plus one active-player diagnostic:** 14 verified publications, four provider failures, one deterministic kickoff abstention. Fourteen of 18 model-invoked workflows completed with publishable stories; the no-call case is not a model success. No cases were skipped and no returned editorial plan was rejected.
- **37 new provider HTTP attempts:** 33 HTTP 200s and four 429s. A successful transport response is not counted as a successful investigation: three failed workflows had one successful request and the unseen-seed failure had two.
- **No unsupported published claim was identified** by the cutoff, evidence-membership and independent hypothesis-recomputation checks or the assistant's reading of accepted outputs. This is limited to the supplied synthetic data and implemented verifier; it is not a universal accuracy or real-world source-quality guarantee.
- **Only one optional model-selected follow-up occurred:** a valid equal-window comparison on the unseen seed. Its final editorial request failed. Every accepted publication used the required first event query and then a story selection. There were **zero explicit counter-evidence investigations** and zero player-specific tool calls. The model could inspect evidence present in the broad query; absence of a follow-up does not reveal its private reasoning.
- **Qualification improved the product output, but the verifier/renderer supplied it.** Declining synthetic xG, opposing shot activity and a fading burst were explicitly qualified. These are real benefits of PR #18's deterministic engine. They must not be described as model-selected counter-investigations.
- **Human preference remains unknown.** All relevance/broadcast scores in this package are assistant preliminary assessments. No football analyst or broadcaster has submitted a review.

## Strong and weak editorial behavior

**Relevance and specificity:** Pressure selections usually lead with the useful shot change. The 65:00 result identifies Milo Serrano and two exact event times, giving a viewer a concrete passage to revisit. At 63:24, the alternative second choice is Theo March's five-pass shot passage. Analyst mode fills three slots and adds an ordinary three-pass pair; distinct evidence does not necessarily mean distinct value.

**Explanatory value and counter-evidence:** The misleading-pressure output explicitly says synthetic xG did not rise despite more shots. The opposing-activity output avoids a one-sided-control conclusion. The fading-burst output notes slowing activity, but still leads with an old wider-window increase after the burst has resolved. These outputs remain numerically defensible while varying substantially in usefulness.

**Evidence quality:** Selected facts survive independent replay and reference visible canonical events. Source limitations remain explicit; substitutions do not become tactical causes, normalized coordinates do not become stadium direction, and absent tracking does not become pressing intensity. The accepted evidence graph is useful for audit. Its size is not a quality score.

**Continuity:** Rewind to 65:00 returned the same selected claims and narrative as the first 65:00 call after future events were removed. That is one useful real repeat plus offline prefix-invariance coverage. It is not proof of universally identical stochastic choices or a browser-session test. At full time, the system preserves the 2–1 score, latest goal and 15–3 shot count, but the model adds a passing passage and an already-fading forward-pass rise instead of a coherent match-ending account.

**Personalization:** The primary favorite was Leon Costa, who had only one recorded pass in the review window. Omitting him was sensible restraint. The added active-Theo diagnostic selected a Theo shot passage, but returned the same narrative and claim IDs as the no-preference Fan case. It demonstrates relevance to a favorite, not incremental personalization or a useful player investigation.

**Repetition and abstention:** The assistant marked two outputs as redundant/overloaded (`opponent-matches` significance text and `pressure-analyst` selection), and one as a likely unnecessary interruption (`quiet-routine`). These are preliminary editorial judgments, not automated factual errors. Zero inappropriate model abstentions were observed because the model never abstained; that does not establish a well-calibrated silence policy.

## Reliability, latency and cost

The deployment was verified at **10 RPM / 10,000 TPM**, unchanged. Cases were spaced at least 65 seconds after the prior request and requests at least 6.1 seconds apart. Four larger workflows still failed: temporal weakening, temporal resolution, familiar comparison seed 8911, and new seed 73129. All failures were provider 429s. No automatic retries, quota increases or model substitutions occurred.

The known estimate is **$0.13567425**, from **183,527 input tokens** (including **16,000 cached**) and **1,962 output tokens**. Four requests returned no usage; their **$0.24420000** reservations remain charged against the allowance. Total conservative accounting is **$0.37987425**, leaving **$1.62012575 and 23 requests unused**. These are token-rate estimates, not billing invoices. The study has been stopped and no remaining allowance is being silently reused.

Including failed attempts, cost per accepted investigation is **$0.009691 known**, or **$0.027134 with unknown-usage reservations**. Workflow median is **7.771 seconds**; observed nearest-rank p95 is **12.859 seconds**, across 18 model-invoked workflows. Request pacing is included and inter-case cooldown is excluded. HTTP-only median/p95 is **1.547/4.721 seconds**, across 37 attempts including failures. The sample is small and correlated; these are not production percentiles. The older comparison used different capacity/pacing, so the latency change is not attributable solely to PR #18.

The append-only, fsynced ledger reserves a conservative byte/token envelope before every HTTP attempt, enforces both $2 and 60 requests, retains unknown usage, and rejects corrupt state or concurrent writers. All **191 pre-existing artifact files** retained their original hashes, including both historical ledgers. The product's production counters were not reset. See [method and preflight](METHOD.md) for pricing sources, source hashes, controls and limitations.

## Baseline conclusion

The frozen PR #18 baseline contains original **1.1.2 deterministic candidates**, not original model responses. The closest saved real 1.1.2 comparison is seed 8911, where the new run failed before publication. Five other human-review examples use actual historical **1.0.2** responses and are explicitly labelled as older-version comparisons in the coordinator materials.

The new engine adds useful qualification and evidence relationships. Several obvious improvements over 1.0.2—named players, event times, better shot selection—already existed in 1.1.x. There is no defensible measured claim that PR #18's model chooses more useful stories, investigates more counter-evidence, or sustains a better narrative than the immediately preceding Director. See [the baseline analysis](BASELINE-COMPARISON.md).

## Final verdict

**PROMISING.** The credible asset is a trustworthy, inspectable event-to-story engine with some useful model selections. It can support an honest recorded hackathon demonstration and exploratory discussion of an editor-assisted product. It has not earned an exceptional-intelligence claim, a dependable live showcase claim or a professional-pilot recommendation.

The highest-value work is to fit complete investigations inside the current operating envelope, suppress stale/routine stories, make counter-evidence materially inform editorial decisions, improve the verified significance/watch wording, and collect independent feedback. Licensed production data, professional validation and operational testing remain necessary before commercial claims. A larger model is not justified by this evidence.

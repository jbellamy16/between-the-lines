Absence of an explicit challenge call does not prove that the model ignored contrary evidence already visible in the broad result. Private reasoning was not inspected. It does mean that the selective counter-evidence workflow is not demonstrated by those cases. More calls would not automatically be better.

These observations do not justify a more expensive model. Better output may come from more selective deterministic eligibility, better use of the current follow-up opportunity and clearer verified rendering. Extra capabilities should be requested only where the underlying event feed cannot answer an important question.

Two further weak outcomes refine that diagnosis. At full time the model selects a passing passage and a fading forward-pass increase, while the server supplies the score, latest goal and total shots. This suggests an **A/D/E** gap in match-ending prioritization and temporal rendering: the recent candidate list supplies isolated observations more readily than a coherent account of earlier developments. The event feed exists; inventing tactical causes or buying a larger model would not address that boundary. In the active-Theo diagnostic, the preference-aware result is identical to the untargeted one, despite available player involvement. This is a **B/D** limitation in demonstrated personalization, not proof of an incorrect selection: the named shot is relevant, but the preference produced no measured incremental value. A future paired test should reward a useful extra contribution only when it adds information.

The full provider-failure set is temporal weakening, temporal resolution, baseline seed 8911 and unseen seed 73129. The unseen seed successfully requested and received an equal-window comparison before its third HTTP request was rate-limited. Its missing final narrative is a provider/packet-size failure, not an editorial rejection. All four deterministic fallback packages are retained, but the direct-investigator harness did not execute the application's catch/fallback path. No historical failure has been silently replaced by a rerun.

## Prioritized plan and acceptance evidence

1. **Run the prepared independent review.** Obtain two football reviews and one broadcast review before tuning prose toward the assistant's taste. Preserve disagreements and distinguish the one available 1.1.2 historical comparison from earlier 1.0.2 examples. Cost: no model calls.
2. **Fit complete investigations to the existing operating envelope, then fix stale-story selection and useful silence.** Reduce duplicated tool-packet text without weakening complete evidence retrieval. Use fading and routine-pass cases as editorial examples; treat the failed larger temporal cases as unmeasured model quality, not assumed editorial failures. Retain cutoff and hypothesis verification. Success: investigations finish, meaningful changes remain visible, resolved/routine activity stops occupying the lead, and blind reviewers prefer the resulting selection. A future live rerun needs a newly authorized allowance.
3. **Make selective counter-evidence materially affect decisions.** Study why the existing optional tool is skipped and whether compact summaries of available challenges would improve selection. Preserve the four-tool/three-request ceiling. Success is a justified change or confirmation in a relevant case, with no extra call in an ordinary self-contained sequence.
4. **Improve the existing verified significance/watch renderer.** Remove fractional-clock artifacts, shared boilerplate and trivial “one more” targets. Do not permit unconstrained tactical prose. Require targeted regressions and label any future version separately from these live receipts.
5. **Validate personalization and professional fit prospectively.** Make an active favorite's meaningful actions useful without forcing an inactive player into a story. Then review a licensed real-match sample with analysts/producers. Success: positive task-specific feedback and useful editorial choices across several matches, not just one synthetic demo.

No new agent framework, general chatbot, model upgrade or application redesign is recommended. Operational sizing is a separate question from football intelligence; higher throughput cannot fix an uninteresting story.

## Commercial readiness

**Fan experience:** The current system can surface a count change and a named replayable sequence, preserve match context and show why stronger interpretations are unsafe. That is a credible companion experience. This study does not show that fans want repeated use, tolerate interruption frequency, understand the caveats, or prefer it to concise conventional statistics. Routine and verbose outputs are practical risks.

**Broadcast experience:** Evidence IDs, match-time bounds and reusable story packages could help a researcher or producer find a recorded passage and check a proposed line. The output still needs an editor: awkward watch criteria, excessive caveats, routine passing selections and incomplete temporal emphasis are not ready for unattended on-air narration. A preview/export is not an integration into a broadcaster's editorial or graphics systems.

**League/club experience:** The architecture demonstrates a constrained, auditable event-to-story workflow with reversible match-time reconstruction. It could support exploratory product discussions about an evidence-linked second screen or editorial tool. Synthetic results do not establish competitive scouting value, coaching insight, commercial demand, adoption or a professional pilot recommendation.

What remains unproven or outside the data:

- Licensed live event access and permission to process, display, replay and redistribute derived content must be agreed for the intended product. This study uses only project-owned synthetic data; it makes no licensing clearance claim.
- Tracking, formations, pressing intensity, player intentions, off-ball influence and causal tactical effects require additional suitable source capabilities and validation. Counts and event-model xG do not substitute for those observations.
- Feed corrections, ordering, competition clocks, missingness, identity resolution and versioned invalidation need production-feed evaluation. Synthetic consistency makes these easier here.
- Authentication, tenant isolation, persistent shared spending controls, operational monitoring, editorial override, retention and dependable concurrency/latency need a separate production readiness exercise. This direct-investigator study does not prove those systems.
- Professional reviewers must validate usefulness in their actual tasks. Commercial discovery must establish buyer, workflow, willingness to pay, integration cost and data economics before forecasting opportunity.

The credible near-term opportunity is an editor-assisted, inspectable football insight workflow. The credible next evidence is independent judgment on real outputs, followed by licensed prospective match testing. Engineering breadth alone does not establish either.

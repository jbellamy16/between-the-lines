## Analysis after feedback arrives

Keep reviewer identities separate from public results. Preserve each original response. Use `coordinator-key.json` to map alternative numbers back to the correct source for that reviewer and case. Check missing scores and ambiguous ties before analysis; do not fill them in automatically.

Report per-case scores, each reviewer's preference and reasons, ties, disagreement, factual objections and unwanted information. Separate the single recorded-1.1.2 comparison from the five exploratory-1.0.2 comparisons. Report the broadcast review separately from the two football reviews. Eighteen case-reviewer observations are not 18 independent matches.

An assistant finding of zero unsupported published claims is not a human truth rating. Resolve any factual objection against the recorded events before discussing average preference. A useful next gate is all three reviewers finding at least one new, worthwhile, correctly qualified development, with no unresolved factual objections and median relevance/broadcast usefulness at least 4/5. This is a proposed pilot gate, not a result or a validated psychometric threshold.
